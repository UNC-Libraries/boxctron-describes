You will be given BASELINE_RESULT and GENERATED_RESULT for the same image. Compare
the full_description, alt_text, transcript, and safety_assessment fields. The baseline
is the reference output, but do not assume it is infallible.

Field purpose:
- full_description: a detailed account of visually observable image content.
- alt_text: a concise, screen-reader-oriented summary of the image's essential content.
- transcript: a faithful transcription of readable text in the image.
- safety_assessment: structured classifications of observable people, sensitive content,
  symbols, text characteristics, image quality, and misidentification risk.

Evaluate material differences in this priority order:
1. Factual faithfulness: flag unsupported assertions or hallucinated details relative to
   the baseline.
2. Completeness: flag omission of important information present in the baseline.
3. Interpretation: flag changed interpretations of people, events, text, objects, or
   safety-relevant content.
4. Quality: assess accessibility, transcription quality, safety classification, conciseness,
   and wording after the higher-priority concerns.

Do not treat stylistic rewording as a difference unless it changes meaning or usefulness.
Without the source image, assess deviation from the baseline rather than factual truth.

Ignore derived statistics and scores. In particular, do not assess or mention risk_score,
inconsistency_count, transcript_statistics, or full_description_transcript_statistics.

Keep every statement concise and specific. In regressions, improvements, and
notable_differences, list only material differences; never list facts that are the same
in both results.

Field purpose:
- regressions: material negative changes in the generated result, such as omitted
  important information, a contradiction, a changed interpretation, or an assertion not
  corroborated by the baseline. State each issue once, only in this field.
- improvements: material gains over the baseline, not unchanged content or speculative
  details.
- notable_differences: material, neutral changes that may help a human compare the
  outputs but are neither regressions nor improvements. Do not repeat an issue already
  listed in regressions or improvements.

Classify the generated result relative to the baseline:
- MATCHES_BASELINE: No material loss or unsupported deviation.
- MINOR_DIFFERENCES: Differences are present but appear non-material.
- POTENTIAL_REGRESSION: Omission, unsupported assertion, interpretation change, or safety mismatch.
- MIXED: Both meaningful gains and potential regressions.
- NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW: The comparison cannot determine whether a material difference is correct.

Provide ONLY the JSON object required by the schema.
